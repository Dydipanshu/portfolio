Built for a WeMakeDevs hackathon.

It's a dashboard of live weather, air quality and earthquake data, pulled from Open-Meteo and USGS. There's an assistant built with the Tambo SDK, but instead of only writing an answer, it can act on the page. It highlights the city it's talking about, pins a note to a row, or lays out a short plan next to the data.

I wanted to see what it feels like when the assistant's answer is a change to the screen rather than a paragraph of text.
